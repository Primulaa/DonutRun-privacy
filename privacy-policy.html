Privacy Policy

Game: «Побег пончика» (Donut Run)
Android application: org.donutrun.game
Last updated: October 14, 2026
Developer: DonutRun
Privacy contact: senchenko.ed@gmail.com

1. Information used by the game

The game does not require registration and does not send user data to the developer or third-party servers. It contains no advertising SDKs, analytics, user tracking, or automatic crash-report uploads. An internet connection is not required for gameplay.

The game does not request access to contacts, location, the camera, the microphone, messages, or personal photos and files. It does not use device advertising identifiers.

2. Information kept on your device

The following information is saved in the application's own storage on your device to provide game features:

  • completed levels, earned stars, collected sprinkles, and best scores;
  • music and sound-effect settings;
  • your selected interface language.

When automatic language selection is enabled, the game reads the system language setting. To display the interface and graphics, the engine uses technical device information such as screen dimensions and graphics capabilities. The engine may also create local technical logs for diagnostics. This information is not automatically sent to the developer.

The records screen shows results saved on this device. It is not an online leaderboard and does not publish your results to other players.

3. Storage, security, and deletion

Saved progress and settings are kept in application storage protected by Android's access controls. The developer has no remote access to this information and does not keep copies on a server. The game does not provide cloud synchronization, and standard Android app-data backup is disabled in the Android build.

Local data remains until you clear the application's data or uninstall the game together with its data. To delete it, open Android Settings → Apps → Побег пончика → Storage → Clear storage / Clear data. Names may vary between devices. Clearing data deletes progress, records, and settings. If Android offers to retain app data during uninstallation, do not select that option if you want to delete all game data.

4. Sharing and third-party services

The game does not share or sell user data. It has no accounts, third-party sign-in, or online leaderboards.

Google Play and the Android operating system may independently process information about app installation, updates, and operation under their own settings and policies. This does not mean the game itself sends information to the developer. Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Support enquiries

If you choose to email the contact address, the developer receives your sender address, message, and any attachments you provide. This information is used to answer your enquiry and resolve the reported issue. Enquiries are handled through the developer's email service, with access limited to what is needed to handle the enquiry.

Correspondence is retained only as long as needed to respond, resolve the issue, and meet applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion by contacting the same email address; information subject to a legally required retention period may be deleted after that period ends.

6. Children's privacy

The game's no-collection and no-sharing practices described above apply to all players, including children. Playing does not require a name, age, or contact details. Children should ask a parent or guardian for help when contacting support.

7. Changes and contact

If game features or data handling change, this policy will be updated and the date above will be revised. Advertising, analytics, accounts, or online features, if introduced, must be reflected in an updated policy.

For privacy questions, contact DonutRun at senchenko.ed@gmail.com.
